Scope limits
- This proves the bounded P2 C12/CLI/bridge projection and expiry behavior for
  the observed production transition only.
- A discovery hint is not identity or authority, an observed lease ACK is not
  permanent authority, and a fresh admission/readiness proof is not a durable
  ResourceGuard grant.
- This proof does not establish arbitrary compute, other providers, LAN access,
  generic RPC, performance, capacity, raw protocol traces, packet captures, or
  unrelated product behavior.
